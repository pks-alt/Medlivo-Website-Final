Privacy & Cookie Policy

Effective November 2025. This policy explains how Medlivo Inc collects, uses, protects, and manages personal information when you use our website, apply for opportunities, or work with our staffing teams.

This policy applies to Medlivo website visitors, job seekers, clinicians, client representatives, and others who provide information through our digital services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, resume or CV, employment history, credentials, licensure, availability, job preferences, and information submitted through forms or applications.

We may also collect technical information automatically, including device and browser information, IP address, pages visited, referring pages, approximate location, and cookie or analytics data.

2. Information from Other Sources

Where appropriate for staffing, employment, credentialing, or compliance, we may receive information from job boards, background screening providers, licensing and credentialing sources, payroll or benefits vendors, workforce platforms, and other service providers.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal information to support recruiting, matching, applications, onboarding, credentialing, employment administration, payroll and benefits, client service, communications, website operations, analytics, security, compliance, and service improvement.

4. How We Share Information

We may share information with healthcare organizations and clients for candidate consideration, with service providers that support screening, credentialing, payroll, benefits, technology, or administration, and with MSP/VMS partners when required for workforce delivery. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect legal rights and security.

Medlivo does not sell personal information.

5. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use essential, analytics, performance, and similar technologies to operate the site, understand usage, and improve user experience. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and, where available, through site consent controls.

6. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or a copy of certain personal information, and to opt out of some marketing communications.

7. Data Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, meet employment and business needs, comply with legal and regulatory requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

9. Third-Party Links

Our site may link to websites or services operated by third parties. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

10. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 through this website.

11. U.S. Website

Medlivo’s website and staffing services are primarily intended for users in the United States. Information may be processed and stored in the United States in accordance with applicable law.

12. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date shown above indicates the current version.
