How Each Division Operates

Built differently for the work each market requires.

The recruiting approach, candidate relationship, credentialing path, and assignment coordination vary by division. Each Medlivo team is structured around those differences.

01
Nursing & Allied

Responsive recruiting across a broad clinical talent market.

Nursing and allied needs can vary by specialty, shift, location, urgency, and client program. The division is structured to move quickly while keeping role-specific requirements in view.

Explore Nursing & Allied →
Talent Areas RN · LPN/LVN · CNA · Imaging · Cath Lab · Respiratory · Laboratory · Surgical Tech · Pharmacy
Recruiting Approach Specialty- and availability-driven outreach across broader talent pools, with attention to shift, location, experience, and client requirements.
Delivery Priorities Qualification, credentialing coordination, program requirements, onboarding, and start readiness.
02
Rehabilitation

Specialized recruiting where care setting and fit matter.

Rehabilitation staffing depends on more than discipline alone. Licensure, patient population, care setting, schedule, location, and clinician preference can all determine whether an assignment works.

Explore Rehabilitation →
Therapy Disciplines PT · PTA · OT · COTA · SLP across inpatient, outpatient, rehabilitation, and post-acute settings.
Recruiting Approach Discipline-specific networks and relationship-driven outreach, with close attention to setting, location, and clinician preferences.
Delivery Priorities Licensure, facility-specific onboarding, therapist-to-setting fit, and assignment continuity.
03
Locum Tenens

Provider coverage managed around specialty, availability, and assignment complexity.

Locum tenens adds layers of coordination beyond sourcing a provider. Specialty, coverage dates, call or clinic expectations, rates, licensing, credentialing, privileging, malpractice, and travel can all affect the assignment.

Explore Locum Tenens →
Provider Types Physicians · Nurse Practitioners · Physician Assistants · CRNAs across primary care and specialty coverage.
Recruiting Approach Direct provider relationships, specialty sourcing, availability windows, and schedule and rate alignment.
Delivery Priorities Licensing, credentialing, privileging, malpractice, travel, and assignment logistics.
One Medlivo Standard

Different operating models. Consistent expectations.

Each division works differently because its market demands it. Across all three, clients and clinicians should expect clear communication, disciplined follow-through, and a team that remains engaged through the assignment.
