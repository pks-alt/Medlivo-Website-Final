Our Story

Built from healthcare staffing, not around it.

Medlivo grew from an established rehabilitation staffing foundation into a broader healthcare staffing platform. That history matters. Rehabilitation taught us early that a job title alone never tells the whole story. The care setting, patient population, schedule, credentials, workload and practical fit all shape whether an assignment works.

As Medlivo expanded into Nursing & Allied and Locum Tenens, we kept that same discipline. Each talent market works differently, so each practice has its own recruiting focus while sharing the operating infrastructure required to deliver consistently.

What We Believe

Accountability should be visible.

Healthcare staffing has too many handoffs for ownership to disappear between them. We build the relationship around clear responsibility from the first conversation through the assignment.

01

Understand the assignment before recruiting it.

Role, setting, schedule and practical expectations matter as much as the title.

02

Treat readiness as part of delivery.

Credentialing, compliance and onboarding are not afterthoughts; they determine whether a start actually happens.

03

Keep a human owner in the process.

Technology can improve speed and visibility. Clients and clinicians still need someone accountable for the next step.

04

Stay connected after the start.

The placement is not finished when someone reports on day one. Assignment support remains part of the relationship.
