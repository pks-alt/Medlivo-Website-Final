Who We Are

Built for healthcare staffing as it actually operates.

Healthcare staffing is not one market. Nursing and allied roles move differently from rehabilitation, and locum tenens introduces its own licensing, credentialing, privileging, and logistical dependencies. Medlivo is structured around those differences rather than forcing every requirement through one generic process.

That specialization is paired with a simple operating expectation: communicate clearly, stay close to the work, and remain accountable through the assignment.
