Our Story

Built inside healthcare. Shaped by the realities of staffing.

Medlivo’s foundation comes from firsthand clinical experience and years spent working inside healthcare staffing.

Healthcare context matters.

Clinical work is specific. Staffing is operational. Finding the right person is only one part of the job. The assignment also has to make sense, the requirements have to be completed, and the clinician has to be ready to start.

Ownership matters too.

The details vary across the clinical markets we serve, but the expectation stays the same: understand the requirement, recognize what could get in the way, and take ownership of moving the work forward.

That mindset continues to shape Medlivo today.

The Market Is Changing

The real question is whether technology makes the work better.

Healthcare organizations expect faster response, better visibility, stronger workforce controls, and more efficient delivery. Clinicians expect a clearer and more connected experience.

At the same time, staffing companies are working across more systems, more data, and more administrative steps than ever before.

More technology is already entering healthcare staffing. We see the opportunity in using it to remove friction, improve visibility, and help experienced teams spend more time on the work that requires judgment.

Our focus Use technology to remove friction and help good teams work better.
Building Medlivo

Technology is part of how we operate.

We use established platforms where they work well. We automate repetitive work where manual effort adds little value. And we build our own tools and intellectual property where existing systems do not solve the problem well enough.

That includes practical uses of AI, workflow automation, and data across the business.

That is where technology creates value for us.
