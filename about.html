About Medlivo

Healthcare staffing with a clinical point of view.

Medlivo supports healthcare organizations and clinicians across Nursing & Allied, Rehabilitation, and Locum Tenens.

Our background in healthcare and staffing influences how we look at the work. We pay attention to the details behind the role, how the assignment will actually function, and what needs to happen for a successful start.

What shapes Medlivo Clinical understanding Operating experience Better use of technology
Our Story

Built inside healthcare. Shaped by the realities of staffing.

Medlivo’s foundation comes from firsthand clinical experience and years spent working inside healthcare staffing.

Clinical work is specific. Staffing is operational. Finding the right person is only one part of the job. The assignment also has to make sense, the requirements have to be completed, and the clinician has to be ready to start.

The details vary across the clinical markets we serve, but the expectation stays the same: understand the requirement, recognize what could get in the way, and take ownership of moving the work forward.

That mindset continues to shape Medlivo today.

How We Are Organized

One healthcare company. Specialized practices.

The clinical markets we serve require different recruiting knowledge, clinical understanding, workflows, timelines, and readiness requirements.

Medlivo keeps that specialization intact while supporting each practice through shared capabilities in credentialing, compliance, onboarding, payroll, client delivery, workforce operations, and technology.

That allows our teams to stay close to their markets while operating with consistency across the company.

The Market Is Changing

The real question is whether technology makes the work better.

Healthcare organizations expect faster response, better visibility, stronger workforce controls, and more efficient delivery. Clinicians expect a clearer and more connected experience.

At the same time, staffing companies are working across more systems, more data, and more administrative steps than ever before.

More technology is already entering healthcare staffing. We see the opportunity in using it to remove friction, improve visibility, and help experienced teams spend more time on the work that requires judgment.

Our focus Make the company more productive, not simply more technical.
Building Medlivo

Technology is part of how we operate.

We use established platforms where they work well. We automate repetitive work where manual effort adds little value. And we build our own tools and intellectual property where existing systems do not solve the problem well enough.

That includes practical uses of AI, workflow automation, and data across the business.

Fewer handoffs Less duplicate work Better visibility Faster decisions More time for clients and clinicians

That is where technology creates value for us.

What Medlivo Stands For

Clinical understanding. Operational discipline. Better execution.

We believe healthcare staffing works best when clinical context, operating discipline, and technology support one another.

Our teams need to understand the markets they serve. Our processes need to be clear and dependable. Our technology needs to make the work easier, faster, and more visible without adding unnecessary complexity.

That is how Medlivo continues to grow: by deepening our healthcare expertise, strengthening the operating model behind it, and using technology where it can make a real difference.
