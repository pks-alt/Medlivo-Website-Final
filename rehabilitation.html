Care Setting Matters

The care setting changes the assignment.

The same discipline can look very different from one care environment to another. Medlivo recruits around the setting, patient needs, workload, documentation, and operating rhythm of the assignment.

01

Acute & Inpatient

Higher acuity, faster decisions, close interdisciplinary coordination, and discharge-driven goals shape the work.

What matters

Setting Experience · Patient Acuity · Schedule · Documentation · Team Coordination

02

Inpatient Rehabilitation

Intensive therapy, measurable functional progress, and coordinated work across rehabilitation disciplines.

What matters

Rehabilitation Experience · Patient Population · Therapy Intensity · Interdisciplinary Care · Schedule

03

Post-Acute & Home-Based Care

Skilled nursing, post-acute, and home-based therapy require continuity, independence, caseload management, and a clear understanding of the patient environment.

What matters

Setting Experience · Caseload · Service Area · Productivity · Documentation · Schedule

04

Outpatient

Scheduled visits where specialty focus, patient mix, throughput, and documentation shape the day.

What matters

Clinical Focus · Patient Population · Caseload · Schedule · Documentation Requirements

How We Recruit

A focused search for your therapy team.

We align the assignment with the therapist’s experience and practical needs before presenting a candidate.

01

02

03

04
