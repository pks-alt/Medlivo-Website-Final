The Rehabilitation Recruiting Lens

Five dimensions define the match.

A viable placement sits at the intersection of clinical capability and practical feasibility. Medlivo evaluates both before a clinician is presented.

THE ASSIGNMENT Can this clinician succeed in this role?

Clinical alignment, workload, geography, licensure, and timing are considered together.

01

Discipline & clinical experience

PT, PTA, OT, COTA, or SLP experience aligned to the clinical demands of the role.

02

Care setting & patient population

Relevant experience in the care environment and with the patient population served.

03

Schedule & workload

Hours, caseload, productivity expectations, assignment length, and availability.

04

Location & mobility

Commute, travel expectations, service area, and the practical ability to work the location.

05

Licensure & start timing

State licensure, required credentials, client standards, and the ability to meet the target start date.

Clinical & Start Readiness

Ready means every critical detail is resolved before day one.

A strong match still depends on execution. Medlivo coordinates professional standing, clinical verification, compliance, onboarding, and final role details before the start.

01

Professional standing

State licensure and discipline-specific credentials verified for the role.

02

Clinical verification

Work history and relevant clinical experience confirmed against the setting and role.

03

Compliance clearance

Required screenings, health documentation, and client- or facility-specific standards completed.

04

First-day readiness

Orientation, schedule, reporting instructions, and final role details confirmed before start.

Why it matters The objective is a clean handoff from selection to first day, with the clinician cleared, informed, and ready to begin.
