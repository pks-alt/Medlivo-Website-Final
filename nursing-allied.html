Inside the Division

Nursing and allied roles require different recruiting expertise.

The work may happen under the same roof, but the experience, credentials, and recruiting signals are not the same.

01 Nursing
Clinical Care

Care delivery roles shaped by unit, specialty, and shift.

From bedside care to coordination and leadership, the right nursing profile depends on unit experience, specialty depth, schedule alignment, and start readiness.

Registered Nurses LPN / LVN CNAs Case Management Care Coordination Nursing Leadership
02 Allied Health
Technical Care

Technical and clinical support roles shaped by modality and environment.

Allied staffing depends on technical specialization, procedure exposure, care setting, and role-specific credentials, not just general clinical experience.

Imaging Cath Lab Respiratory Laboratory Surgical Tech Pharmacy Medical Assistants
How We Recruit

Start with the role. Recruit to the details.

Our team builds the search around what will actually determine whether a clinician can step into the assignment successfully.

01

Clinical fit

Specialty, unit, modality, technical background, and relevant experience.

02

Schedule & location

Shift, assignment structure, geography, travel expectations, and availability.

03

Credentials

Licensure, certifications, documentation, and client-specific requirements.

04

Start readiness

Whether the clinician can complete the required steps within the target timeline.

Clinical Readiness

From selection to first shift, the details still matter.

Once a clinician is selected, Medlivo coordinates the steps required to get the assignment ready to start.

01

Professional standing

Licensure, certifications, and role-specific eligibility confirmed for the opening.

02

Clinical verification

Experience, skills, and work history verified against the role.

03

Compliance clearance

Health, screening, background, and client- or program-specific standards completed.

04

First-shift readiness

Orientation, schedule, reporting instructions, and final start details confirmed.

The goal A clinician who arrives cleared, informed, and ready to work.
