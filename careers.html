Working at Medlivo

Ownership matters here.

Healthcare staffing moves quickly, and good execution depends on people who communicate clearly, solve problems, and follow through. Medlivo is building teams that are comfortable taking ownership and working across functions when the situation requires it.

We value practical judgment, responsiveness, and people who care about the quality of the outcome , not just completion of the task.
