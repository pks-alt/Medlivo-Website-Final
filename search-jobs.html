Working with Medlivo

What happens after you find a job.

From your first recruiter conversation through your assignment, we keep the next step clear and make sure you know who to contact.

01 Review the opportunity

Talk through the role, location, schedule, available pay information, start timing, and requirements before you decide to move forward.

02 Complete credentialing

Get help with licenses, documents, compliance items, onboarding, and anything needed to become assignment-ready.

03 Get ready to start

Confirm your start details, reporting instructions, assignment contacts, and what to expect on your first day.

04 Stay supported

Keep a Medlivo contact throughout your assignment so you know where to go when questions or needs come up.
