Care Setting Matters

The care setting shapes the role.

Patient needs, clinical responsibilities and team support vary from one practice to another. We clarify those differences before beginning the search.

01

Hospital & Inpatient Care

Hospital-based work varies by patient acuity, admissions, rounding responsibilities and the support available across each shift.

What matters

Patient acuity · Service responsibilities · Shift structure · Team support

02

Emergency & Urgent Care

Patient volume, the range of conditions treated and available resources shape the experience needed for each opening.

What matters

Patient volume · Acuity · Procedures · Available resources

03

Surgery, Anesthesia & Procedural Care

Relevant experience must match the cases performed, the provider’s responsibilities and the way the clinical team works.

What matters

Case mix · Procedure experience · Care-team model · Call expectations

04

Primary Care & Specialty Practice

Clinic-based roles depend on patient population, appointment schedules, specialty focus and continuity of follow-up.

What matters

Clinical focus · Patient population · Visit volume · Follow-up responsibilities

How We Recruit

A focused search for the right provider.

A dedicated contact coordinates the search, interviews and updates with your hiring team, including MSP and VMS program requirements.

01

Understand the need

Confirm the specialty, clinical responsibilities, coverage or hiring model, and target start.

02

Find relevant experience

Identify physicians and advanced practice providers whose recent work fits the role.

03

Confirm mutual fit

Discuss schedule, call expectations, location and compensation, including long-term practice fit for permanent roles.

04

Present the candidate

Share relevant experience, qualifications and availability, then coordinate interviews and feedback.
